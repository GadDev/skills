# Roadmap — Workshop Registration (V1)

Source: `feature.txt`. Sequencing below is the source's own; nothing else is committed.

## Before building
- Resolve the identity contradiction (see `constitution.md`).
- Choose a backend and database.
- Why first: both shape every later phase. They are open, not scheduled work.

## Phase 1 — Pilot: registration and capacity, one workshop
- One maintainer, roughly two weekends.
- Participants register with name and email.
- Organiser-set capacity is enforced.
- Duplicate email for the same workshop is prevented.
- Why first: the source says to prove registration and capacity handling first; these address the double-registration and lost-places pains.

## Phase 2 — Organiser view and CSV export
- Organisers view registrations and export them as CSV.
- Why second: the source says export is added after the pilot.
- `[ASSUMPTION — confirm]` Access control for organisers is needed here.

## Unplaced V1 requirements
- `[OPEN]` Placement of the 30-day deletion: in the pilot or after it. The source lists it as V1 but gives no phase.
- `[OPEN]` Placement of listing multiple upcoming workshops (the pilot uses one workshop).

## Not in V1
- Payments and calendar integrations.
- Waitlists: not committed ("later" at most).

We organise monthly engineering workshops and currently handle registrations
through messages and a spreadsheet. People sometimes register twice, and
organisers lose track of available places.

We already have a React + TypeScript website. Keep that frontend.

For V1:
- Participants can see upcoming workshops and register.
- Each workshop has a capacity set by the organiser.
- Prevent duplicate registrations for the same email and workshop.
- Organisers can view registrations and export them as CSV.
- Collect only name and email.
- Registration data must be deleted 30 days after the workshop.
- One maintainer, roughly two weekends to build the pilot.
- First prove registration and capacity handling with one workshop.
  Add organiser export after that.
- Payments and calendar integrations are outside V1.

We haven't chosen a backend or database. Someone suggested Firebase,
but that isn't a decision.

Participants shouldn't need an account.
Also, only authenticated employees should be allowed to register.

Waitlists could be useful later, but we haven't committed to them.
